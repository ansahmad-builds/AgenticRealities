What it is

Less scattered research.
More considered choices.

Bongaus is a creator-research workspace for consumer brands and agencies. It combines multilingual discovery across YouTube and publicly indexed TikTok profiles with saved evidence, recommendations, and outreach preparation.

01 / Discovery

Look beyond one language.

Search across a saved multilingual research library. Filter known size metrics, organise creators with tags, and build a shortlist around your brief.

02 / Research

See why a creator fits.

Review content themes, buying guidance, and potential concerns with source evidence. YouTube captions can add timestamped passages when available.

03 / Recommendations

Build on what you learn.

Follows, marks, and notes inform recommendations. Keep the explanation beside each suggestion instead of treating a ranking as a final decision.

04 / Outreach

Prepare a relevant introduction.

Review contact leads, personalise a draft using saved content, assign an owner, and record the next action. Export the shortlist for your team’s existing process.

How it works

From a search
to a shared shortlist.

  1. 1

    Discover.

    Gather candidate creators through multilingual terms and supported public sources.

  2. 2

    Inspect.

    Open saved content and evidence. Distinguish measured performance, creator-reported information, and missing data.

  3. 3

    Shortlist.

    Follow, tag, and annotate candidates. Keep the team’s reasoning and research history together.

  4. 4

    Prepare.

    Review the contact and pitch, choose a next action, and copy or export the preparation. Bongaus does not send email.

Public search snippets are leads to verify. Creator-supplied audience figures are labelled as reported; unavailable metrics remain unknown.

For the people behind
